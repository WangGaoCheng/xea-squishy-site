Buyer FAQ & Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
Is XEA a direct squishy toy factory or a trading intermediary?

XEA operates with direct factory production and warehousing in Jinhua, Zhejiang, giving global importers direct access to 25+ ready-to-ship crunchy block molds, 200+ mini mochi molds, and in-house OEM tooling.

Why do global wholesalers choose XEA over 500-pc minimum factories?

Most competing factories require 500 pcs per individual shape even on stock molds. XEA lowers your entry barrier to 1 master carton (144–192 pcs per shape) so you can test more designs with less inventory risk.

What regions does XEA export to?

XEA regularly ships to toy importers, wholesalers, Amazon FBA sellers, and promotional agencies across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia.

How can I verify quality before placing a bulk order?

You can order a mixed stock sample kit (ships in 3–5 business days) or schedule an independent SGS/Intertek AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection at our Jinhua facility.
