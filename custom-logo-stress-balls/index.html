Custom Logo & Promotional Stress Ball FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom printed stress balls with our logo?

For stock PU foam, TPR, and silicone shapes with a 1-color pad-printed company logo, MOQ starts at just 500 pieces (1,000 pieces for full-color UV printing or custom Pantone body colors). Custom 3D shaped molds start at 3,000 pieces.

Which printing method lasts longest on squeezable TPR, silicone, and PU foam stress balls?

Flexible elastic pad printing with cured silicone/TPR ink bonds directly into the outer skin so logos will not crack or peel when squeezed. For multi-color gradients on flat surfaces like butter bars or 26-key fidget keyboards, we use flexible UV digital printing.

Do custom logo stress balls come with non-toxic US safety documentation?

Yes. All XEA promotional stress balls and squishy toys are 100% water-bead-free, phthalate-free, and tested to ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, and US CPC standards so schools, hospitals, and corporate HR teams can distribute them safely.

How fast can you produce and deliver trade show stress balls to the USA or Europe?

Stock shapes with custom 1-color logo printing take 7 to 10 business days in production plus 5 to 8 days via Express Air DDP—meaning event planners can receive branded cartons in under 18 days door-to-door.

What artwork file format do you need for personalized stress balls?

Send vector artwork in AI, EPS, PDF, or high-resolution SVG along with your Pantone (PMS) color codes. Our Jinhua graphics desk returns a free digital proof within 24 hours before any plate is made.

Can we order custom stress balls for schools, hospitals, and dental offices?

Yes! Our top institutional promotional molds include the 125g Crunchy Brain Stress Ball (healthcare/tech), Big White Tooth Squishy (dental clinics), Non-LED 26-Key Quiet Fidget Keyboards (schools/offices), and slow-rising PU foam shapes.

Can you pack branded stress balls inside custom event boxes or employee wellness kits?

Yes. We supply individual frosted gift boxes, clear polybags with custom insert cards, or multi-item corporate wellness kits combining a logo stress ball and a fidget keyboard.

How much do printing setup plates cost for logo stress balls?

Standard 1-color pad-printing setup plates are $45 to $75 one-time per logo color (waived on orders of 5,000+ pcs). There is $0 mold fee when printing on any of our 150 stock shapes.
