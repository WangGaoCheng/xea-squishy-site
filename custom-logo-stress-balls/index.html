Custom Logo Stress Balls Wholesale FAQ

What printing method lasts longest on squeezable stress balls without cracking?

Elastic two-part polyurethane pad printing cured through a 65°C infrared drying tunnel achieves over 180% elongation recovery and resists cracking across 2,000+ squeezes on PU foam, sugar gel TPR, and silicone shapes. For flat surfaces like cubes or 26-key keyboards, we use flexible full-color UV digital printing.

What artwork file format and Pantone specs do you need for a free 2-hour virtual proof?

Send vector artwork in AI, EPS, PDF, or editable SVG format with fonts converted to outlines, plus your exact Pantone (PMS Solid Coated) color codes. Our Jinhua graphics team checks imprint sizing and returns a scaled digital mockup within 2 working hours at $0 charge.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ), and can I mix shell colors?

Custom logo pad printing starts at 500 pieces per design using any of our 150+ ready-to-ship stock shapes ($0 custom mold fee). If your logo plate prints cleanly across multiple stock body colors in the same size, you can split 500 pieces across 2 stock colors.

How fast can XEAtoy deliver custom printed stress balls to a US or EU trade show?

For rush booth orders of 500 to 3,000 pieces in clear OPP pouches, production wraps in 5 to 6 days and Express Air DDP reaches your US or EU exhibition address in 5 to 7 days, giving you reliable 10 to 14-day door-to-door turnaround after artwork sign-off.

Are physical pre-production samples available, and is the sample fee credited back?

Yes. For standard orders, we shoot HD photo and squeeze-video proofs of your first printed piece off the press at no delay. If your marketing team requires physical DHL samples before bulk run approval, sample setup costs are 100% credited toward orders of 1,000+ pieces.

Do you support neutral blind drop-shipping and NDA protection for promo agencies?

Yes. XEAtoy routinely partners with ASI, PPAI, and European promotional distributors under strict mutual NDA. All blind-shipped orders use plain unbranded master cartons with your agency shipper details on packing lists, and we never contact your end corporate client.
