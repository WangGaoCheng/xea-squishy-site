Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
How many mini mochi squishy molds does XEA keep in stock?

XEA maintains 200+ ready-to-run mini mochi TPR molds across animals, marine life, dinosaurs, unicorns, glitter editions, and holiday themes so buyers can curate no-duplicate assortments without new tooling fees.

Are XEA mini mochi squishies individually wrapped to prevent sticking?

Yes. Every mini mochi squishy receives a light food-grade anti-tack coating and is sealed in an individual OPP bag before being packed into PDQ boxes, FBA value bags, or master cartons.

What safety certifications apply to XEA bulk mochi squishies?

Manufactured in Jinhua, Zhejiang, all XEA mochi squishies are 100% water-bead-free, phthalate-free, and support ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and EN71 Parts 1–3 compliance.

What is the lead time for seasonal Christmas or Easter mochi packs?

Custom seasonal assortment packs typically require 7–12 days for pre-production samples and 15–25 days for mass production.
