Wholesale Fidget Keyboard Sourcing FAQ

What is the MOQ for wholesale fidget keyboards and clicker keychains?

Our stock trial MOQ is just 1 master carton (120 pcs for the 10-LED and Non-LED 26-key models, or 144 pcs for the 26-LED full-light model). For custom OEM keycap colorways, custom laser-printed logos, or private-label color boxes, MOQ starts at 1,000 units per design.

Do your light-up LED fidget keyboards comply with US CPC, ASTM F963-23, and Reese's Law battery safety?

Yes. All XEA fidget keyboards are tested to ASTM F963-23, CPSIA lead/phthalate limits, and EN71 Parts 1-3. For LED models, battery compartments use secured screw-fastened closures compliant with US CPSC button-cell / Reese's Law requirements, with full US CPC certificates provided for Amazon FBA and retail buyers.

What mechanical switch feel do your 26-key clicky keyboard fidget toys use?

We assemble tactile clicky mechanical-style switches calibrated for crisp audible feedback (55-60gf actuation force) and rated for over 50,000 presses. Quiet-switch or softer-actuation options are available for OEM classroom and office sensory orders.

Can I mix 26-LED, 10-LED, and Non-LED fidget keyboards in one trial shipment?

Yes. Importers and Amazon FBA sellers routinely order 1 carton of each tier (total 384 pcs) to A/B test price points across TikTok Shop, Amazon, and gift retail before scaling to full-container replenishment.

What packaging options are available for Amazon FBA and retail hanging displays?

Every unit can ship in clear OPP bags with FNSKU barcode stickers, translucent frosted gift boxes, retail blister cards, or 12-pc counter PDQ display boxes. Custom die-cut header cards start at 1,000 pcs.

How fast is lead time from Jinhua to US or EU warehouses?

Ready-to-ship stock cartons dispatch within 3 to 5 business days from our Jinhua warehouse. Air DDP to US/EU FBA warehouses takes 7 to 12 days; fast sea DDP takes 22 to 32 days.

Can XEA customize keycap legends (letters, emojis, brand logos, or Pantone colors)?

Yes. We support custom Pantone ABS/PBT keycap molding, pad-printed brand icons, pastel/macaron/cyberpunk color palettes, and custom keychain lanyards for promotional corporate giveaways and licensed retail programs.

Do you offer mini 4-key or 9-key keyboard fidget keychains in addition to 26-key full boards?

Yes. Alongside our flagship 26-key full-alphabet clicker keyboards (26-LED, 10-LED, Non-LED), our molding line supplies 4-key linear clickers and mini keychain keyboard fidgets on OEM/bulk order. Request our full clicker catalog in your RFQ.
