Buyer FAQ & Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
Why should US importers replace water bead stress balls with maltose gel or PU foam?

Because US CPSC enforcement restricts expandable polymer water beads due to ingestion hazards, XEA supplies 100% water-bead-free maltose gel stress balls, dough stress balls, and slow-rising PU foam squishies that clear Amazon CPC and customs safely.

What is the MOQ for custom logo promotional stress balls?

Pad-printed logo customization on stock PU foam, gel, or TPR stress balls starts at 500–1,000 pcs directly from our Jinhua, Zhejiang factory, with 7–12 day sample turnaround.

Do your slow-rising PU foam squishies support custom scents?

Yes. We offer optional light vanilla, strawberry, peach, or bread scents using non-toxic toy-safe fragrances that meet EN71 and ASTM F963-23 migration limits.

How long is the rebound time on XEA slow-rising stress balls?

Our slow-rising PU foam line rebounds smoothly over 8–15 seconds, while our high-viscosity maltose gel stress balls provide dense, silent slow-knead resistance for adult desk and sensory relief.
