Jumbo Flour Dough & Viral Animal Squishy FAQ

What makes flour dough squishies different from regular gel or PU foam squishies?

Flour dough squishies use a dense, moldable food-grade flour/dough compound sealed inside an ultra-elastic TPR shell. Unlike liquid gel, they hold sculpted shapes when squeezed and deliver a heavy, satisfying 200g-400g sensory weight.

What is the MOQ for jumbo 350g Axolotl and 400g Fried Drumstick squishies?

Because jumbo SKUs are heavier (350g-400g each), 1 master carton is just 24 to 48 pieces (20-26 kg GW). That makes it effortless to test jumbo novelty SKUs with low upfront capital.

Do you have Highland Cow squishies in multiple fillings?

Yes! Highland Cow is one of the highest-searched squishy shapes in the US (18,100+ monthly searches). XEA stocks Highland Cow across 3 textures: Slushie (200g), Pearl Gel (133g), and Crunchy Building Block TPR.

Can I order Christmas and Halloween squishy toys with custom retail header cards?

Yes. Our Christmas Face-Changing Dog (XEA-V2-107), Halloween Face-Changing Dog (XEA-V2-119), and Christmas Butter Bar are ready in stock at 96-100 pcs/ctn, with custom seasonal header cards available from 1,000 pcs.

How does the Clownfish Blind Box squishy work for retail and claw machines?

XEA-V2-103 (250g, 13.5x12.5 cm) ships 72 pcs/ctn inside opaque mystery blind boxes, creating repeatable unboxing excitement for specialty toy stores, party favor buyers, and arcade redemption counters.

Are flour dough squishies safe and compliant for US and European toy standards?

Yes. Every flour dough and jumbo squishy in our catalog is non-toxic, phthalate-free, and certified to US CPC, ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, and EU EN71 standards.

What is the shelf life of flour dough stress balls in warehouse storage?

Stored in cool, dry cartons away from direct sunlight, our double-sealed TPR flour dough squishies maintain peak softness and elasticity for 18 to 24 months.

Can XEA develop a custom jumbo animal or food shape for my private label?

Yes. Custom 3D sculpting and steel mold fabrication for jumbo dough toys takes 15-20 days, with OEM production starting at 2,000 units.
