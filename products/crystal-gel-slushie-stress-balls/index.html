Crystal Gel & Slushie Stress Ball Wholesale FAQ

Are XEA crystal clear gel and slushie stress balls 100% water-bead-free?

Yes. Every single Crystal Gel, Pearl Gel, and Slushie stress ball is manufactured with 100% bead-free food-grade maltose/sugar gel, pearlescent gel, or crushed-ice TPR slushie filling. We never use expanding polymer water beads, eliminating US CPSC recall risk.

What is the difference between Crystal Clear Gel, Pearl Gel, and Slushie filling?

Crystal Clear Gel delivers ultra-transparent slow-rebound squish showcasing internal roses, ribbons, or fruit slices. Pearl Gel adds a silky metallic shimmer with creamy resistance. Slushie & Ice-Crush filling creates a crunchy snow-cone / crushed-ice tactile feel with soft ASMR sound.

What is the wholesale MOQ for glitter, rose cube, and slushie stress balls?

Stock trial MOQ is 1 master carton per SKU (96 to 192 pcs depending on unit weight, typically 20-27 kg per carton). Mixed-carton trial packs are available for qualifying importers.

Do transparent gel stress balls yellow or leak in warm shipping containers?

We use thick-walled 1.6-1.8 mm UV-stabilized TPR membranes with ultrasonic/thermal double-sealing and conduct 48-hour 45C thermal-chamber compression tests prior to shipment to prevent yellowing and seam leaks.

Which shapes have the highest retail velocity in US and EU markets?

Our top-moving shapes are the Rose Cube Stress Ball (XEA-V2-023), Crystal Butter Bar (XEA-V2-034, 230g), Slushie Highland Cow (XEA-V2-005, 200g), Slushie Glitter Ball (XEA-V2-006), and Pearl Gel Capybara & Unicorn.

Can I order custom glitter colors, scented gel, or private-label color boxes?

Yes. Custom glitter flake colors, translucent tints, custom header cards, and FBA-ready 3-pack/4-pack window gift boxes start at 1,000 units per SKU.

What toy safety certificates come with each shipment?

Every SKU in this 39-piece line carries valid US CPC, ASTM F963-23, CPSIA (lead & phthalate free), and EU EN71-1/2/3 documentation.

How many cartons fit in a 20GP container or LCL pallet?

Standard cartons measure roughly 30x45x35 cm (0.047 CBM, 21-26 kg). A standard 1.2x1.0 m pallet holds 24-30 cartons (~3,500-4,300 pcs), and a 20GP holds ~550 cartons.
