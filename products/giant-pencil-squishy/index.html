Wholesale Sourcing FAQ

What is the MOQ for wholesale giant and mini pencil squishies?

Just 1 master carton: 48 pcs for the Jumbo Giant Pencil (23 kg) or 240 pcs for the 15 cm Mini Dough Pencil.

Can we print our school or brand logo on the pencil shaft?

Yes! Custom 1-color pad printing along the yellow barrel starts at 1,000 pieces.

Yes. Non-toxic flour dough and TPR shells carry ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, and US CPC certification.

Yes, we ship mixed cartons from our Jinhua warehouse in 3-5 business days.
