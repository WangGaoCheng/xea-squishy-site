Why is the capybara squishy one of the top-performing animal SKUs?

Capybara squishy searches exceed 12,100 monthly queries in the US alone, driven by viral kawaii gift trends. XEA stocks capybara crunchy block squishies at 144 pcs/ctn for immediate wholesale fulfillment.

Can XEA produce custom animal mascots or licensed characters?

Yes. Our Jinhua, Zhejiang engineering team converts 2D sketches or 3D STP files into exclusive custom animal squishy molds starting at 3,000 pcs, with 7–12 day prototyping under NDA.
