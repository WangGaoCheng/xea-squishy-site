Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
Why is the capybara squishy one of the top-performing animal SKUs?

Capybara squishy searches exceed 12,100 monthly queries in the US alone, driven by viral kawaii gift trends. XEA stocks capybara crunchy block squishies at 144 pcs/ctn for immediate wholesale fulfillment.

Can XEA produce custom animal mascots or licensed characters?

Yes. Our Jinhua, Zhejiang engineering team converts 2D sketches or 3D STP files into exclusive custom animal squishy molds starting at 3,000 pcs, with 7–12 day prototyping under NDA.

Are XEA animal squishies suitable for claw machines and kids' party favors?

Yes. Our 144-pc crunchy block animals have consistent 140g–180g weights ideal for medium claw machines, while our individually bagged mini mochi animals fit party goodie bags and capsule vending.

What toy safety certifications cover your animal squishy toys?

All XEA animal squishies are 100% water-bead-free and engineered for ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, EN71 (Parts 1–3), and CE compliance.
