Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
What makes platinum-cure taba squishies different from regular TPR squishies?

Platinum-cure food-grade silicone delivers a super-soft, creamy 6–8 second slow rebound with zero oil bleeding or harsh chemical odor, making it the preferred material for viral TikTok cat paw taba squishies.

How does XEA prevent taba silicone squishies from collecting dust?

Buyers can select either a soft velvet flocking finish or heavy-gauge crystal OPP squish bags designed so end consumers can squeeze the taba squishy directly inside the sealed bag.

What is the MOQ for custom taba squishy shapes or colors?

Color and packaging customization on XEA's existing cat paw, animal, and dessert taba molds starts at 500 pcs, while exclusive custom 3D silicone molds start at 3,000 pcs.

Are XEA silicone squishy toys safe for US and European retail?

Yes. All XEA taba squishies are 100% water-bead-free, phthalate-free, and produced in Jinhua, Zhejiang to meet ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and EN71 Parts 1–3 standards.
