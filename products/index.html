What is the minimum order quantity to buy squishies in bulk from XEA?

Stock crunchy building block squishies start at just 1 master carton (144 pcs per SKU, or 192 pcs for Big White Tooth). Light OEM logo or packaging customization starts at 500–1,000 pcs, and custom 3D molds start at 3,000–5,000 pcs.

Can I mix multiple collections in a single squishies bulk buy order?

Yes. You can mix individual stock cartons across fruit, food, animal, and novelty shapes, or consolidate crunchy block TPR, taba silicone, slow-rising PU foam, and mini mochi packs into one shipment from our Jinhua warehouse.

Do you offer cheap bulk squishies for claw machines, schools, and party favors?

Yes. Our mini mochi TPR assortments (200+ stock molds) and 192-pc carton Big White Tooth offer the lowest per-unit factory pricing for arcades, vending capsules, Easter eggs, and classroom prize bins.

Are all XEA bulk squishy toys 100% free of expandable water beads?

Yes. Every product across all 8 collections is 100% water-bead-free to comply with strict US CPSC enforcement and protect Amazon US sellers from listing removals.

What toy safety certifications cover your wholesale squishy toys?

XEA production supports EN71 (Parts 1–3), ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and CE compliance, with phthalate-free formulas and AQL 2.5 pre-shipment quality inspection.

How do plain and emoji-face editions affect wholesale pricing and MOQ?

Most of our 25+ stock building block molds are stocked in both realistic plain and kawaii emoji-face editions at 144 pcs/ctn, with only a minimal per-unit print differential.

Can XEA prepare Amazon FBA bundles, FNSKU stickers, and PDQ retail boxes?

Yes. Starting at 500 sets for custom gift boxes or 1 carton for FNSKU sticker prep, our factory handles multi-pack bundling, suffocation warnings, and FBA carton labeling.

How fast do stock sample kits and bulk orders ship from Jinhua?

Mixed stock sample kits dispatch in 3–5 business days via DHL/FedEx/UPS. Standard bulk orders ship in 15–25 days, and custom 3D mold runs require 25–35 days.

Which Incoterms and shipping methods do you support?

We quote EXW Jinhua, FOB Ningbo or Shanghai port for container buyers, and door-to-door DDP air (7–10 days) or DDP ocean freight (25–35 days) straight to US, UK, EU, and CA warehouses.
