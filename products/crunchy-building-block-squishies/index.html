01 · Can I mix different block squishy shapes in a 1-carton trial?
Standard wholesale cartons contain 144 pcs (or 192 pcs for Big White Tooth) of one SKU, while mixed-SKU sample packs are available in 3–5 days so you can test multiple shapes before ordering full cartons.
02 · What is the price difference between Plain and Emoji Face editions?
Emoji-printed versions add a small pad-printing charge (typically ~$0.04–$0.05 per unit) across 24 core molds, giving retailers higher shelf impulse appeal at minimal extra cost.
03 · Are these crunchy block squishies suitable for claw machines and party favors?
Yes, unit weights of 104g–194g and individual OPP bags make them top performers for claw machine toys bulk, party favor squishies, and retail gift counters.
04 · Are the internal mini blocks safe under US CPSC rules?
Yes, our block-filled TPR line is 100% water-bead-free and complies with ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, and EN71 Parts 1–3 for US and European distribution.

Competitor Trap #1: 500-Pc Per Shape MOQ

Most Jinhua & Yiwu factories force 500 pcs per mold, locking $3,000+ in untested shapes.

XEA Shield: Start at 1 full carton (144 pcs; Big White Tooth 192 pcs) per shape.
Competitor Trap #2: Banned Water-Bead Fillings

Expandable polymer beads trigger US CPSC recalls and instant Amazon ASIN removals.

XEA Shield: 100% solid bead-free clay & mini-block core engineered for ASTM F963-23 & CPC.
Competitor Trap #3: Thin TPR Seam Blowouts

Cheap thin-wall skins split within 20 squeezes, causing 1-star FBA reviews and refund spikes.

XEA Shield: Reinforced wall thickness + AQL 2.5 seam pull testing on every production batch.
Competitor Trap #4: Sticky Oil Bleeding in Transit

Unstabilized TPR sweats plasticizer oil during warm ocean transit, staining retail boxes.

XEA Shield: Matte anti-dust surface finish + sealed crystal OPP bag on every single unit.
