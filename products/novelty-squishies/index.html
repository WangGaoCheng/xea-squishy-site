Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
How does XEA's thermochromic color-changing squishy work?

Our color-changing durian uses safe heat-sensitive thermochromic pigment blended into the TPR outer shell; body warmth triggers a vivid color shift while squeezing, then reverses cleanly as it cools.

Why does Big White Tooth pack 192 pcs per carton instead of 144 pcs?

Big White Tooth has a slightly more compact mold footprint, allowing 192 pcs per export master carton—lowering per-unit ocean freight cost for dental clinics, promo agencies, and wholesalers.

Can I order 1 carton each of multiple novelty squishies to test TikTok Shop?

Yes. XEA's 1-carton stock MOQ (144–192 pcs per SKU) lets e-commerce sellers test viral ASMR and novelty squishies with minimal capital risk.

Are XEA novelty squishies compliant with US and EU toy standards?

Yes. Every novelty SKU is 100% water-bead-free and manufactured at our Jinhua, Zhejiang factory to meet ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and EN71 standards.
