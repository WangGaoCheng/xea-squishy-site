MOQ & Pricing

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale squishy toys?

Ready-to-ship stock SKUs start at 1 master carton (24 to 144 pcs per design, depending on toy size), letting Amazon FBA and retail buyers test real sell-through before scaling. Custom packaging or logo pad printing starts at 500 pcs, and custom 3D OEM molds start at 3,000 pcs.

MOQ & Pricing

Can I mix multiple squishy designs and fillings in one trial shipment?

Yes. Most first-time importers combine 5 to 15 full master cartons across crunchy block TPR, taba platinum silicone, crystal slushie stress balls, jumbo flour dough squishies, and 26-key LED fidget keyboards in one consolidated Air DDP or Sea LCL shipment.

MOQ & Pricing

How do your wholesale FOB price tiers work?

We quote transparent 3-tier FOB Ningbo/Yiwu pricing: Tier 1 (1-carton trial / 48–288 pcs), Tier 2 (500–2,999 pcs wholesale), and Tier 3 (3,000–50,000+ pcs container or OEM volume). Request our 2026 Catalog PDF below to receive exact per-SKU carton math within 24 hours.

MOQ & Pricing

Do you offer samples before I place a bulk wholesale order?

Yes. Stock sample kits ship via DHL/FedEx express in 3 to 5 business days. You only cover the small sample and courier fee, and 100% of your sample cost is credited back onto your first wholesale production order.

MOQ & Pricing

What payment terms do you accept for B2B wholesale and OEM orders?

For ready-to-ship stock orders under $3,000, payment is 100% prior to dispatch (3–5 day turnaround). For custom packaging or OEM production runs, standard terms are 30% T/T deposit to start production and 70% balance after you approve pre-shipment inspection photos/videos.

OEM & Custom Molds

How much does a custom 3D squishy mold cost in 2026?

Factory platinum silicone master molds for taba squishies cost $450 to $900 (7–10 day sample lead time), CNC aluminum PU foam molds cost $600 to $1,400, and multi-cavity P20 steel TPR injection molds run $1,200 to $3,800. We rebate 100% of steel mold fees once cumulative volume reaches 30,000 pcs.

OEM & Custom Molds

Can you print my company logo on promotional stress balls?

Yes. Our dedicated Custom Logo Stress Balls line supports 1-to-4 color pad printing, UV full-color digital printing, and debossed mold logos starting at 500 pcs per shape, using flexible abrasion-resistant inks that do not crack when squeezed.

OEM & Custom Molds

Will you sign an NDA to protect my custom squishy shape?

Yes. We sign bilateral NDAs prior to receiving your 3D STL/STEP files or sketches. Dedicated OEM steel and silicone molds are stored in locked client racks and are never shown in our public catalog or sold to other buyers.

OEM & Custom Molds

What custom retail packaging options do you manufacture?

Starting at 500 to 1,000 pcs, we produce custom-printed window display boxes, 12-unit counter PDQ display trays, opaque foil mystery blind bags, 24-day advent calendar boxes, and thick 0.08 mm play-in-bag OPP pouches with English/EU care insert cards.

Safety & Compliance

Are your wholesale squishy toys 100% water-bead-free?

Yes. Every single SKU in our 150+ catalog is 100% water-bead-free. We strictly use safe solid or paste cores: food-grade maltose sugar gel, cornstarch flour dough, crushed-ice polymer slushie flakes, platinum liquid silicone, or slow-rising PU foam.

Safety & Compliance

Do you provide US CPC, ASTM F963-23, and CPSIA certificates for Amazon FBA?

Yes. All stock wholesale SKUs are manufactured from virgin phthalate-free TPR and food-grade platinum silicone with current ASTM F963-23, CPSIA Lead/Phthalate, and US CPC documentation from CPSC-accepted third-party labs (SGS, Intertek, CTI).

Safety & Compliance

Are your squishies compliant for European Union (EN71 / CE) and UKCA markets?

Yes. We supply EN71-1 (mechanical/physical), EN71-2 (flammability), and EN71-3 (19 migrating heavy metals) test reports along with CE marking and EU Responsible Person label placement on custom packaging runs.

Safety & Compliance

Do your TPR or silicone squishies have a strong chemical smell?

No. Chemical odor happens when workshops blend cheap recycled TPR regrind or excess mineral oil. We mold strictly with sealed 25 kg sacks of virgin food-grade TPR pellets and vacuum-degassed platinum silicone so toys arrive odor-neutral.

Safety & Compliance

What quality control standard do you run before shipping?

Every production lot passes in-house 90-Newton seam pull tests, 31.7 mm small-parts choke-cylinder checks, and ISO 2859-1 AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection. We also welcome third-party inspectors (QIMA, SGS, Intertek) at our Jinhua workshop prior to balance payment.

FBA, Packing & DDP

Can you apply Amazon FNSKU barcodes and suffocation warnings at the factory?

Yes. We apply FNSKU unit labels, polybag suffocation warnings, and Amazon FBA box ID labels directly on our Jinhua packing line for ~$0.02/pc, saving you $0.50+ per unit in US third-party prep center fees.

FBA, Packing & DDP

Do you offer door-to-door DDP shipping to the US, UK, EU, and Australia?

Yes. We ship FOB Ningbo/Yiwu for importers with their own forwarder, or handle turnkey Air DDP (7–11 days) and Sea DDP (25–35 days) straight to your warehouse or Amazon FBA fulfillment center with all customs duties prepaid.

FBA, Packing & DDP

How do you prevent gel and dough stress balls from crushing or stiffening in transit?

We pack in heavy-duty 5-ply K=K export cartons capped at 14–16 kg gross weight (Amazon FBA 50 lb compliant) with internal cardboard divider layers. For winter transit below 5°C, our glycerin-stabilized gel and dough return to normal softness within 4 hours at room temperature.

FBA, Packing & DDP

What is your lead time for stock orders vs. custom OEM orders?

Stock wholesale orders dispatch in 3 to 5 business days. Custom packaging or logo pad-printed orders ship in 10 to 15 days. New custom 3D OEM molds require 7 to 18 days for tooling plus 15 to 20 days for mass production.
