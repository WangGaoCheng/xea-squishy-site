4 Custom Toy Packaging Mistakes — Prevented by XEA

Mistake #1: Missing Mandatory Warning Icons

Boxes printed without CPSIA tracking lots or EU CE/UKCA marks get detained at customs.

XEA Shield: Our Jinhua pre-press team verifies all US/EU compliance icons before plates are cut.
Mistake #2: Crushed Thin Gift Boxes in Sea Freight

250g thin paperboard collapses during humid 30-day ocean container transit.

XEA Shield: 350g laminated board + 5-ply export master cartons preserve retail shelf corners.
Mistake #3: Rigid Ink Peeling Off Stress Balls

Cheap screen-printing cracks after 10 squeezes on PU foam or TPR shells.

XEA Shield: Flexible phthalate-free pad-print inks cured specifically for squeeze toys.
Mistake #4: Packaging & Toy Schedule Mismatch

Sourcing boxes from a separate printer delays container loading by 2 weeks.

XEA Shield: In-house packaging coordination in Jinhua synchronizes box & toy completion.

Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
What is the lowest MOQ for private-label packaging at XEA?

Printed OPP pouches, custom header cards, FNSKU labels, and direct logo pad printing start at just 500–1,000 pcs directly from our Jinhua, Zhejiang factory.

Does XEA provide blank packaging dielines for our graphic designer?

Yes. Once you select your squishy SKU and box or pouch format, we email print-ready AI/PDF dielines within 24 hours at zero charge.

Can XEA print mandatory US/EU compliance warnings directly on the packaging?

Yes. We include age-grading (3+), choking hazard warnings, tracking lot numbers, CE/UKCA marks, and Amazon suffocation warnings directly in your print artwork.

Will pad-printed logos crack when buyers squeeze the stress balls?

No. XEA uses high-elasticity, phthalate-free toy inks formulated specifically for flexible TPR, PU foam, and silicone surfaces, verified under ASTM F963-23 and EN71 guidelines.
