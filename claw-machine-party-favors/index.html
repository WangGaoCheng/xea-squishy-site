Are XEA crunchy block squishies the right weight for commercial claw machines?

Yes. At 120g–180g per piece with structured block shapes, our crunchy TPR squishies grip reliably in standard medium claw machines, while our 15g–25g mini mochi squishies fit mini claws and 50mm vending capsules.

Can XEA supply logo-printed promotional stress balls for tight event dates?

Yes. Stock stress ball shapes with 1–4 color logo pad printing ship in 15–20 days from our Jinhua, Zhejiang factory, with expedited air express available for urgent trade shows.

Are party favor squishies individually bagged for hygienic distribution?

Yes. Every unit ships sealed in a clean individual OPP bag so event planners, schools, and retailers can drop them straight into goodie bags or prize bins.

Tiered factory pricing unlocks automatically at 500, 1,000, 3,000, and 10,000+ units—contact our export desk for an exact volume quotation.
