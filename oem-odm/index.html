Custom OEM & Stress Ball Manufacturing FAQs

01 · Do you offer customized stress balls no minimum or personalized stress balls no minimum?
As a direct B2B factory, we do not print 1-piece retail orders, but we bridge low-volume buyers with 1-carton (144 pcs) stock trials and low 500-pc light OEM logo runs—far below standard 2,000-pc factory minimums.
02 · How do you prevent logo ink from cracking when squeezing custom printed stress balls?
We pre-treat PU and TPR surfaces and apply flexible, stretch-cured pad-printing ink that flexes with the toy skin without flaking or peeling during repeated compression.
03 · What file formats do I need to submit to open a new 3D custom mold?
Send STEP, IGES, STL, or even 2D AI/PDF mascot sketches; our Jinhua engineering team converts 2D concepts into 3D printable prototypes for your approval within 5–7 days.
