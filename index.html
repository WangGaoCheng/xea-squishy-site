01What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale squishy toys?
XEA’s ready-to-ship stock MOQ is 1 export carton (144 pcs for most crunchy block SKUs, or 192 pcs for Big White Tooth). Light customization such as Pantone colors, logo printing, or custom packaging starts at 500–1,000 pcs per design, while brand-new OEM molds start at 3,000–5,000 pcs.
02Can I mix Plain and Emoji Face versions in one wholesale order?
Yes, 24 of our ready-to-ship fruit and food block molds are stocked in both classic Plain and Kawaii Emoji Face editions. You can combine full cartons of different shapes or order mixed-SKU sample packs to compare both versions before placing volume reorders.
03Are XEA crunchy block squishies 100% water-bead-free?
Yes, 100% of XEA crunchy building block squishies are manufactured completely bead-free without expandable polymer water beads. We use safe non-swelling mini blocks and sensory clay fillings sealed inside elastic TPR skins to comply with US CPSC, ASTM F963-23, and EU EN71 standards.
04Which toy safety certifications can XEA support for US and EU imports?
XEA maintains full compliance readiness for EN71 (Parts 1–3), CE, ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, and Amazon CPC documentation. All TPR, silicone, maltose gel, and PU foam materials are phthalate-free, and we coordinate third-party lab testing whenever private-label orders require SKU-specific certificates.
05How do I order a mixed-SKU sample pack before committing to bulk?
Submit your target SKUs through our RFQ form below, and our Jinhua export team will ship a mixed sample pack within 3–5 days via DHL or FedEx. Sample fees are credited back toward your first confirmed bulk wholesale order.
06Can you produce custom printed stress balls or custom squishy maker OEM lines?
Yes, XEA serves as a full custom squishy maker for custom printed stress balls, corporate giveaways, and exclusive 3D character molds. We support pad printing, Pantone color matching, and custom tooling under mutual NDA protection.
07What are the standard export carton sizes and gross weights?
Standard block squishy cartons hold 144 pcs (or 192 pcs for Big White Tooth), measuring 41.5×29×31.5 cm to 60×45×50 cm with gross weights between 16 kg and 28 kg. Every quotation includes exact CBM and gross weight for landed freight calculation.
08Do you offer cheap bulk squishies for claw machines and party favors?
Yes, we supply cheap bulk squishies tailored for claw machine operators, carnival prize buyers, and party favor squishies distributors. Mini mochi TPR packs and 144-pc block cartons deliver factory-direct margins with individual OPP hygiene packaging.
09How do you prevent TPR squishies from getting sticky or attracting dust?
Our TPR formula minimizes plasticizer migration, and every unit receives a dry-touch anti-dust coating before sealing inside an individual OPP bag. This keeps inventory spotless through humid sea transit and extended warehouse storage.

We offer suffocation-warning OPP bags, window color boxes, blind bags, and 12-to-24-count PDQ retail display trays. For Amazon FBA sellers, our Jinhua warehouse applies FNSKU barcode stickers and carton shipping labels prior to dispatch.
