Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
Can XEA ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses in the US, UK, or EU?

Yes. Our Jinhua export team applies individual FNSKU barcodes, suffocation warnings, and Amazon box labels on the production line and ships via DDP air or ocean straight to your assigned FBA fulfillment centers.

How does XEA help prevent Amazon toy listing suspensions?

All XEA squishies are 100% water-bead-free, phthalate-free, and engineered to meet ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and EN71 Part 1–3 standards required for Amazon toy gating.

What is the smallest test order an FBA or TikTok Shop seller can place?

Stock crunchy building block squishies start at just 1 master carton (144 pcs per shape), while custom multi-pack FBA gift boxes start at 500 sets.

Do you provide high-resolution product photos or carton dimensions for FBA fee calculators?

Yes. Every quote includes exact master carton dimensions, gross weight (16–28 kg), unit weight, and studio product visuals so you can calculate exact FBA referral and fulfillment fees upfront.
