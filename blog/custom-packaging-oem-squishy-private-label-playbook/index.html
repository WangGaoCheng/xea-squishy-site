Private Label Squishy Packaging & Custom 3D Molding: 2026 Factory Cost & Timeline Playbook

By XEA Jinhua Engineering Team Last reviewed: November 2026 6 min read
Key Takeaway (Direct Answer)

Brands can launch private-label squishies in two tiers at XEA: Light OEM (custom logo, Pantone color, OPP bag, PDQ tray, or window gift box) starting at 500-1,000 pcs in 15-25 days, or Full 3D Custom Character Molding starting at 3,000-5,000 pcs in 25-35 days under NDA.

Light OEM vs. Full 3D Custom Molding: Which Tier Fits Your Launch?

Not every brand needs to pay for brand-new steel or aluminum tooling on day one. Understanding the two customization tiers at our Jinhua factory helps you protect cash flow while still building an unmistakable retail brand.

4 High-Converting Retail Packaging Formats

  • PDQ Tear-Top Display Tray: Holds 12 or 24 individually bagged units for instant checkout-counter placement in toy and convenience chains.
  • Clear PET Window Gift Box: Ideal for 4-pack and 6-pack Amazon FBA gift sets with full CMYK branding and mandatory ASTM/EN71 compliance icons.
  • Mystery Blind Foil Pouch: Drives repeat impulse purchases for TikTok live unboxing and collectible kawaii series.
  • Matte Frosted Zip Bag: Reusable anti-dust storage bag loved by sensory and taba silicone buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does XEA sign NDAs for exclusive 3D squishy molds?

Yes. All custom 3D STP files, character sketches, and exclusive molds paid by the client are protected under mutual NDA and reserved exclusively for your orders.

Can you provide free packaging dielines before I pay a deposit?

Yes. Tell us which stock SKU and box or pouch style you want, and our packaging engineers will send print-ready AI/PDF dielines within 24 hours.

What file formats do you need to quote a custom shape?

We accept 3D STP/OBJ/STL files, Adobe Illustrator vector sketches, or even clear reference photos with target dimensions in millimeters.
