How to Clean a Taba Squishy in 4 Safe Steps (Without Ruining the Silicone)

The fastest, safest way to clean a taba squishy is rinsing it under lukewarm running water (25°C to 38°C) with one drop of mild, dye-free dish soap, rubbing gently with clean fingertips, and air-drying on a smooth glass or silicone mat. Never scrub platinum silicone with paper towels, alcohol wipes, or hot water above 50°C.

  1. Rinse Loose Lint First: Hold the squishy under gentle lukewarm tap water for 10 seconds to flush away loose hair and pocket dust before rubbing.
  2. Use 1 Drop of Mild Dish Soap: Gently massage the surface with your fingers. Dish soap lifts skin oils and grime without attacking platinum silicone bonds.
  3. Rinse Thoroughly & Shake Dry: Rinse until zero soap film remains. Shake off water droplets, or dab gently with a clean, lint-free microfiber cloth (never fuzzy bath towels or tissue paper, which shred and stick instantly).
  4. Finish Based on Your Texture Preference: Leave it bare if you love the signature sticky taba feel, or lightly brush with food-grade cornstarch if you prefer a silky, matte, non-sticky surface.

Can You Really Fix a Torn or Broken Taba Squishy?

You can repair a small clean surface tear (under 10 mm) on a solid platinum silicone taba squishy using neutral-cure clear silicone adhesive clamped gently for 24 hours. Honest limitation: If a hollow gel-filled TPR squishy splits open and leaks its internal maltose or slushie core, or if a solid taba squishy rips cleanly in half, discard it. No household glue can safely re-seal pressurized gel cavities to meet ASTM F963-23 child-safety pull standards.

How to Store Taba Squishies (Retail Shelf & Bulk Warehouse Rules)

Proper storage prevents yellowing, dust contamination, and surface flattening during 12-to-18 month retail cycles. Whether you hold 50 units in a boutique or 10,000 units in an FBA warehouse, follow these four rules:

  • Seal Individually in 0.08 mm OPP or Frosted EVA Bags: Bare taba squishies pressed together in bulk bins fuse slightly over time and pull pigment across colors. Every piece in our taba silicone squishy wholesale line ships individually bagged.
  • Keep Below 32°C (90°F) and Out of Direct Sunlight: Prolonged UV exposure yellows crystal-clear silicone and fades pastel neon pigments within 60 days.
  • Never Stack Heavy Cartons Above 5 High: Soft Shore 00-10 silicone compresses under heavy static loads. Use 5-ply export cartons with cardboard inner trays so bottom-layer cat paws stay plump.
  • Separate Flocked vs. Glossy SKUs: Flocked fuzzy squishies shed fine nylon micro-fibers in transit if packed open next to bare glossy silicone toys.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make my taba squishy sticky again?

Wash the taba squishy in lukewarm water with 1 to 2 drops of mild dish soap to remove skin oils, lint, and cornstarch residue. Rinse completely and let it air-dry on a clean glass plate for 20 minutes; its original natural silicone tackiness returns immediately.

How do I stop a taba squishy from being sticky?

Lightly dust the clean, dry squishy with **food-grade cornstarch** (or talc-free baby powder) using a soft makeup or pastry brush, then tap off the excess. Alternatively, keep the squishy inside its clear 0.08 mm OPP play pouch while squeezing.

Can you wash a flocked (fuzzy) taba squishy with water?

No. Soaking a velvet-flocked taba squishy in soap and water loosens the electrostatic flocking adhesive and creates bald patches. Clean flocked squishies dry using a soft-bristle brush or light tape dabs.

Why does my taba squishy smell slightly sweet or oily?

High-grade platinum silicone is nearly odorless, though some retail sellers add a light food-safe vanilla or milk fragrance oil. If a squishy smells sharply like chemicals or burns your eyes, it is likely low-grade uncured silicone or recycled TPR and should not be used by children.
