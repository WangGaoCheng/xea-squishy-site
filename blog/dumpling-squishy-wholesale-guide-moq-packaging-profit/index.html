Dumpling Squishy Wholesale Guide (2026): MOQ, Steamer Basket Packaging & Retail Profit Math

By XEA Jinhua Engineering Team Last reviewed: November 2026 8 min read
Key Takeaway (Direct Answer)

Wholesale dumpling squishies cost $0.48 to $1.65 FOB China depending on size (5 cm mini to 10 cm jumbo), filling (flour dough, maltose gel, or platinum silicone), and steamer basket packaging. Adding a mini plastic or bamboo steamer basket raises FOB cost by $0.22 to $0.45 but lifts US retail pricing from $4.99 to $11.99-$14.99, delivering 65% to 74% gross margin after DDP freight.

Few novelty stress toys have held steady US search demand for five straight years like the dumpling squishy (baozi stress ball). Unlike flash-in-the-pan viral shapes that crash after 60 days, dumpling squishies sell year-round across Asian grocery chains, boutique gift shops, claw machines, museum stores, and Amazon FBA bundles.

Still, plenty of first-time importers leave half their margin on the table by ordering the wrong filling or skipping steamer-basket packaging. Below is our 2026 factory breakdown of dumpling squishy wholesale specs, MOQs, packaging options, and real landed profit math from our Jinhua production floor.

Steamer Basket Packaging: Plastic vs. Natural Bamboo vs. Blind Bag

Packaging is the single biggest profit lever on a dumpling squishy. A bare dumpling in an OPP bag looks like a $3.99 impulse toy; the exact same dumpling tucked inside a 9.5 cm mini steamer basket commands $11.99 to $14.99 on Amazon and in boutique gift shops.

"If you ship sea freight to humid US coastal warehouses, pick PP faux-bamboo plastic steamers over raw woven bamboo unless you pay for kiln-dried fumigation and silica gel packs. Raw bamboo absorbs container condensation during 30-day ocean transit and can sprout mildew spots on arrival." — Chen Wei, Packaging & Export Engineer, XEA Jinhua Factory

Honest limitation: Steamer baskets add roughly 20% to 25% volumetric weight (CBM) to your master cartons. If you ship exclusively by Air Express DDP, bulky bamboo steamers eat into air-freight margins; switch to sea DDP for orders above 500 steamed sets, or use flat-packed blind bags for fast air restocks.

3 Ways Top Importers Multiply Average Order Value (AOV) on Dumplings

Selling a single dumpling squishy works well in brick-and-mortar impulse racks, but online sellers capture much stronger ad ROI by bundling:

  1. The "Dim Sum 3-Pack" Gift Set: Pack 3 assorted colors (white classic bao, pink peach bao, gold glitter dumpling) inside one 12 cm steamer basket. Your landed cost rises by only ~$1.45, while your retail price jumps from $12.99 to $19.99–$22.99.
  2. 12-Unit Countertop PDQ Display Box: For independent toy stores and Asian markets, pre-pack 12 mini steamer dumplings in a tear-off counter PDQ tray. Retail store buyers reorder PDQ displays 3x faster than loose bulk cartons because staff open the box and place it straight by the register.
  3. Mystery Color Blind Bags: Seal 6 pastel and glitter dumpling variants inside opaque foil pouches. Buyers routinely purchase 3 to 5 bags per checkout chasing the "rare gold glitter" dumpling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale dumpling squishies?

Stock dumpling squishies start at **1 master carton (72 pcs for 8.5 cm standard dumplings, or 144 pcs for 5 cm mini dumplings)**. Custom steamer belly bands, mystery foil bags, or custom Pantone dumpling colors start at **500 to 1,000 pcs**.

Do wholesale dumpling squishies come with US CPC and ASTM F963-23 certificates?

Yes. All XEA dumpling squishies use 100% water-bead-free flour dough, maltose sugar gel, or platinum silicone and ship with valid **ASTM F963-23**, **CPSIA**, and **EN71** lab reports for US and EU customs clearance.

Which dumpling filling sells best: flour dough or sugar gel?

**Flour dough** looks and feels most like real steamed bread dough and has the lowest unit cost ($0.68–$1.05). **Maltose sugar gel** (especially with gold or blue glitter) has higher durability and wins on Amazon FBA and TikTok Shop where visual sparkle drives clicks.

Can I order a mixed carton of classic white, pastel, and glitter dumplings?

Yes. We offer assorted-color export cartons so you can test 4 to 6 dumpling variants inside a single trial shipment without committing to 1,000 pieces per color.

How long does production take for custom-packaged dumpling squishies?

Ready-to-ship stock cartons dispatch within **3 to 5 business days**. Custom-printed steamer belly bands, retail window boxes, or mystery blind bags take **12 to 15 days** from artwork approval. ---
