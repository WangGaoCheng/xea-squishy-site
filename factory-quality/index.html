Buyer FAQ & Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
Where is XEA's squishy toy factory located and which ports do you ship from?

XEA operates directly in Jinhua, Zhejiang, China—one of China's primary sensory toy manufacturing hubs—with fast export trucking to Ningbo and Shanghai ports for FOB, CIF, and DDP air or ocean shipments.

Are XEA squishy toys 100% free of expandable water beads?

Yes. Every XEA squishy line—including crunchy building block TPR, platinum taba silicone, maltose gel stress balls, mini mochi, and slow-rising PU foam—is 100% water-bead-free to meet strict US CPSC and Amazon safety enforcement.

What safety standards and inspection levels does XEA support?

XEA production supports EN71 (Parts 1–3), ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, and CE toy safety compliance, backed by AQL 2.5 pre-shipment quality inspection covering seam pull strength, weight tolerance, and packaging integrity.

Can buyers request third-party lab testing or pre-shipment inspection?

Yes. Importers may book SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas pre-shipment inspections at our Jinhua facility or request batch-specific third-party lab test reports for private-label SKUs.
