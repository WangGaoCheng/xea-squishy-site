Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Last reviewed: November 2026 · XEA Jinhua Factory
How does XEA adapt MOQ and logistics for different B2B buyer models?

Operating directly in Jinhua, Zhejiang, XEA bridges low-MOQ e-commerce testing (144 pcs/ctn stock) and full container OEM/ODM manufacturing (3,000+ pcs custom molds) under one roof.

Can XEA support multi-SKU container consolidation?

Yes. Importers can combine crunchy block TPR, platinum taba silicone, slow-rising PU foam, maltose gel stress balls, and mini mochi packs in a single FOB Ningbo/Shanghai shipment.

Are all XEA wholesale solutions compliant with US and EU toy laws?

Yes. Every line is 100% water-bead-free and manufactured to meet ASTM F963-23, CPSIA, CPC, EN71 Parts 1–3, and CE compliance.
